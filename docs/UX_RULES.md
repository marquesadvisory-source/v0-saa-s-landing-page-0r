# MA&I UX Rules

1. Apply the 30-second comprehension rule.
2. Design mobile first and keep typography readable.
3. Show no more than two primary calls to action per viewport.
4. Keep residence and investments distinct, but connect them when an applicant needs an investment.
5. Use “Investment Opportunities,” not “Become an Investor” or “Investments Ready.”
6. Never imply that Marques is the government, a law firm, or a guarantee of approval.
7. Never mark an asset as residence-eligible without documented review.
8. Use verified facts and controlled legal language.
9. Qualify leads progressively through the appropriate Zoho Form.
10. Preserve routing, multilingual content, analytics attribution, and future API integration.

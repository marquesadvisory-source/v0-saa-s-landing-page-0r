# Marques Advisory & Investments — Website Product Brief

## Positioning

Marques Advisory & Investments operates at the intersection of Costa Rica residence coordination and selected real-asset investment opportunities. It is not presented as a law firm, an immigration authority, or a mass-market property portal.

The commercial architecture has two clear paths:

1. **Advisory — Become a Resident in Costa Rica.** Investor, pensioner, and rentier pathways; program guide; private enquiry.
2. **Investments — Explore Investment Opportunities.** Selected real estate, real assets, and structured opportunities; investment enquiry.

The residence and investment journeys connect when an investor applicant needs a qualifying asset. Investments must also remain relevant to investors who are not seeking residence.

## Experience rules

- The homepage explains who Marques is, what it does, and how it helps in one glance.
- The campaign phrase “Become a Resident in Costa Rica” must be immediately recognizable.
- A visitor must identify a relevant path and next action within 30 seconds.
- Use no more than two primary commercial choices in the opening viewport.
- Residency content prioritizes pathway, process, requirements, and enquiry.
- Investment content prioritizes opportunities, asset facts, suitability review, and enquiry.
- Do not invent client testimonials, performance claims, eligibility, inventory, or government outcomes.
- “Sovereign Portfolio” may support the firm’s philosophy later; it is not the primary entry point.

## Brand and delivery

- Official name: **Marques Advisory & Investments** (no accent).
- Visual direction: editorial, private-client, dark navy, ivory, restrained gold, real Costa Rica imagery.
- Languages: English (default), Spanish, French, and Simplified Chinese. Each view must contain only its selected language.
- Architecture: separate routes, reusable content, API-ready opportunity data, responsive and accessible components.
- Source of truth: GitHub. Review through a Vercel preview before production.
- Lead capture: separate Zoho Forms for residence enquiries and investment/real-estate enquiries.
